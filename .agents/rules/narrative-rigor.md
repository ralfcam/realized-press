# Realized Press: Narrative & Literary Rigor Harness Guidelines

**Project Harness Prompt & Agentic Guardrails (Antigravity Integration)**

These guidelines codify the high-leverage narrative, structural, and epistemic principles required to elevate rigorously audited research dossiers into landmark works of nonfiction across all Realized Press series (*Parenthood: The Atlas*, *Architectures of Memory*, and future publications).

---

## Directive I: Cure "Module-Tour Syndrome" via Concentric Movement Architecture

* **The Principle:** Avoid encyclopedic lists of sequential research modules. Chapters must not read as mechanical tours through 14–19 disconnected sub-topics.
* **The Harness Rule:**
  1. Structure every chapter into **4 to 5 thematic movements** anchored by a unifying conceptual narrative.
  2. Frame each chapter with a concrete opening and closing human or sensory scene (e.g., Chapter 1’s ultrasound flicker) to ground abstract mechanisms in lived reality.
  3. Ensure that internal section transitions function as causal handoffs (e.g., *if placental regulation ends, what regulates the newborn? $\to$ relationships*), preserving narrative momentum across paragraph blocks.

---

## Directive II: Replace "Negative-Boundary Fatigue" with the 3-Tier Causal Resolution

* **The Principle:** Repeatedly ending sections with defensive methodological disclaimers (*"However, observational studies are confounded..."*) dulls reader engagement and creates negative-boundary fatigue.
* **The Harness Rule:**
  1. Transform repetitive cautionary disclaimers (*"What this chapter must not infer"*) into **positive explanatory insight** by deploying the **3-Tier Causal Resolution**:
     * **Tier 1:** What changes the average trajectory of a population (public health / policy signals).
     * **Tier 2:** What meaningfully changes an individual child's or system's trajectory (conditional mechanisms).
     * **Tier 3:** What parents or operators can realistically control (actionable art & judgment).
  2. Use effect sizes, confidence intervals, and attenuation data to explain *why* population averages do not act as deterministic individual forecasts.

---

## Directive III: Embed "Then vs. Now" Landmark Experiment Set-Pieces

* **The Principle:** Landmark studies compressed into 1–2 meta-analytic sentences lose their historical drama and human stakes.
* **The Harness Rule:**
  1. Feature **one full-scene narrative set-piece (300–500 words)** per chapter examining a foundational experiment.
  2. Structure the narrative set-piece across four beats:
     * **The Original Quest:** What researchers were trying to discover and how the experiment was designed.
     * **The Historical Orthodoxy:** What contemporaries concluded at the time.
     * **The Replication & Revision:** How later replications, preregistered consortia (e.g., ManyBabies), or stronger causal designs (sibling comparisons, IVF cross-fostering) overturned or bounded the finding.
     * **The Modern Consensus:** What we can reasonably conclude today.

---

## Directive IV: Enforce Progressive Lens Escalation (Study Deduplication)

* **The Principle:** Re-introducing flagship studies (e.g., BEIP, PROBIT, $11\beta\text{-HSD2}$, Hart & Risley) from scratch across multiple chapters creates redundant text and narrative drag.
* **The Harness Rule:**
  1. Track recurrent empirical anchors across chapters using **progressive lens escalation**:
     * **First Appearance:** Introduce study design, population, and biological/relational mechanism.
     * **Second Appearance:** Reference the prior baseline in a single clause and shift exclusively to structural/ecological constraints.
     * **Third Appearance (and beyond):** Focus strictly on intervention scalability, attenuation, fadeout, or long-term persistence.

---

## Directive V: Maintain Balanced Evidentiary Density Across All Lenses

* **The Principle:** Asymmetry between technical/biological chapters (heavily backed by OKF evidence cards) and organizational/philosophical chapters (relying on general notes) weakens overall manuscript authority.
* **The Harness Rule:**
  1. Enforce a minimum threshold of **12 to 15 OKF v0.2 Evidence Cards per book** across all series.
  2. Ensure that Organizational and Philosophical chapters (e.g., *Architectures of Memory* Ch 09–16) possess the same rigorous primary-source card backing (`evidence-cards/*.md`, locked DOIs, zero retractions) as technical machine-learning chapters.

---

## Directive VI: Decouple Visual Print Syntax from Oral TTS Syntax (ARC-62)

* **The Principle:** Markdown tables (`table`), bulleted lists (`li`), biochemical symbols (`11β-HSD2`), and statistical parentheticals (`RR 0.31`) optimized for printed books and visual readers break the auditory spell when synthesized verbatim by Gemini TTS.
* **The Harness Rule:**
  1. Utilize the BWE dual-representation block architecture where applicable:
     * **`display_html`:** Preserves exact Markdown tables, specialized symbols, and footnote superscripts (`[^slug]`) for visual reader editions.
     * **`spoken_text`:** Normalizes symbols into natural spoken English (*"eleven-beta H-S-D-two"*, *"a sixty-nine percent relative risk reduction"*) and converts comparison tables into flowing prose while preserving exact block IDs and zero-drift PCM duration synchronization.
