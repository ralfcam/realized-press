# Realized Press: Narrative & Literary Rigor Harness Guidelines

**Project Harness Prompt & Agentic Guardrails (Antigravity Integration)**

These guidelines codify the high-leverage narrative, structural, and epistemic principles required to elevate rigorously audited research dossiers into landmark works of nonfiction across all Realized Press series (*Parenthood: The Atlas*, *Architectures of Memory*, and future publications).

---

## Directive I: Cure "Module-Tour Syndrome" via Concentric Movement Architecture

* **The Principle:** Avoid encyclopedic lists of sequential research modules or exposed outline scaffolding. Chapters must not read as mechanical tours through 14–19 disconnected sub-topics or expose internal template beats as section headings.
* **The Harness Rule:**
  1. Structure every chapter into **4 to 5 thematic movements** (`## I. ...` through `## V. ...`) anchored by a unifying conceptual narrative.
  2. Frame each chapter with a concrete opening and closing human or sensory scene (e.g., Chapter 1’s ultrasound flicker, Chapter 2’s familiar spoken cadence) integrated directly into the prose—never under a literal `## The puzzle` or `## Return` subheading.
  3. Dissolve competing hypotheses (`Competing explanations`) organically into the opening movement rather than isolating them as a bulleted pedagogical exercise.
  4. Ensure that internal section transitions function as causal handoffs (e.g., *if placental regulation ends, what regulates the newborn? $\to$ relationships*), preserving narrative momentum across paragraph blocks.

---

## Directive II: Organic Integration of "The Reader's Contract" & "What This Does Not Tell Us" (3-Tier Causal Resolution)

* **The Principle:** Isolating epistemic boundaries into standalone segments (`## The Reader's Contract`, `## What this chapter does not tell us`, `## What the Evidence Does Not Say`, `## Science, Interpretation, Art`) breaks narrative immersion, creates repetitive appendix-fatigue, and treats epistemic discipline as an afterthought rather than the living method of the prose.
* **The Harness Rule:**
  1. **Zero Standalone Epistemic/Contract Segments:** Never use standalone subheadings, meta-tables, or end-of-chapter bullet lists for `The Reader's Contract`, `Science, Interpretation, Art`, or `What this chapter does not tell us`.
  2. **In-Situ Narrative Integration:** Weave the distinction between **Science** (what is observed, measured, replicated), **Interpretation** (how findings are contextualized in systems), and **Art** (human judgment, relational values, lived experience)—as well as what a finding *refuses to license*—directly into the narrative moments where each mechanism or study is introduced.
  3. **Deploy the 3-Tier Causal Resolution in Prose:** Whenever resolving a contested empirical claim, replace defensive disclaimers with positive explanatory insight across three tiers:
     * **Tier 1 (Population Signal):** What shifts the average trajectory across a population (public health / policy fortifications).
     * **Tier 2 (Individual Mechanism):** What conditional biological, genetic, or ecological pathways mediate an individual child's or system's trajectory—and why unadjusted observational correlations attenuate under stronger designs.
     * **Tier 3 (Actionable Art & Judgment):** What parents or operators can realistically control in lived relationship without turning daily life into an optimization performance.

---

## Directive III: Embed "Then vs. Now" Landmark Experiment Set-Pieces

* **The Principle:** Landmark studies compressed into 1–2 meta-analytic sentences lose their historical drama and human stakes.
* **The Harness Rule:**
  1. Feature **one full-scene narrative set-piece (300–500 words)** per chapter examining a foundational experiment, woven directly into one of the chapter's core movements.
  2. Structure the narrative set-piece across four beats:
     * **The Original Quest:** What researchers were trying to discover and how the experiment was designed.
     * **The Historical Orthodoxy:** What contemporaries and popular culture concluded at the time.
     * **The Replication & Revision:** How later replications, preregistered consortia (e.g., ManyBabies), or stronger causal designs (sibling comparisons, IVF cross-fostering) overturned or bounded the finding.
     * **The Modern Consensus:** What we can reasonably conclude today.

---

## Directive IV: Enforce Progressive Lens Escalation (Study Deduplication)

* **The Principle:** Re-introducing flagship studies (e.g., BEIP, PROBIT, $11\beta\text{-HSD2}$, Hart & Risley) or repeating identical methodological refrains (*"A sensitive window is not an inevitable-injury window"*) across multiple chapters creates redundant text and narrative drag.
* **The Harness Rule:**
  1. Track recurrent empirical anchors across chapters using **progressive lens escalation**:
     * **First Appearance:** Introduce study design, population, and biological/relational mechanism.
     * **Second Appearance:** Reference the prior baseline in a single clause and shift exclusively to structural/ecological constraints.
     * **Third Appearance (and beyond):** Focus strictly on intervention scalability, attenuation, fadeout, or long-term persistence.

---

## Directive V: Maintain Balanced Evidentiary Density Across All Lenses

* **The Principle:** Asymmetry between technical/biological chapters (heavily backed by OKF evidence cards) and organizational/philosophical chapters (relying on general notes) weakens overall manuscript authority.
* **The Harness Rule:**
  1. Enforce a minimum threshold of **12 to 15 OKF v0.2 Evidence Cards per book** across all series.
  2. Ensure that Organizational and Philosophical chapters (e.g., *Architectures of Memory* Ch 09–16) possess the same rigorous primary-source card backing (`evidence-cards/*.md`, locked DOIs, zero retractions) as technical machine-learning chapters.

---

## Directive VI: Decouple Visual Print Syntax from Oral TTS Syntax (ARC-62)

* **The Principle:** Markdown tables (`table`), bulleted lists (`li`), biochemical symbols (`11β-HSD2`), and statistical parentheticals (`RR 0.31`) optimized for printed books and visual readers break the auditory spell when synthesized verbatim by Gemini TTS.
* **The Harness Rule:**
  1. Prefer flowing literary prose over interrupting Markdown tables or mechanical bullet lists inside chapter movements.
  2. Utilize the BWE dual-representation block architecture where applicable:
     * **`display_html`:** Preserves exact typography and footnote superscripts (`[^slug]`) for visual reader editions.
     * **`spoken_text`:** Normalizes symbols into natural spoken English (*"eleven-beta H-S-D-two"*, *"a sixty-nine percent relative risk reduction"*) while preserving exact block IDs and zero-drift PCM duration synchronization.
