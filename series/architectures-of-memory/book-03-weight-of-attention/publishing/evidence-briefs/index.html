Fixed-Vector Bottleneck in Sequence-to-Sequence Models and Soft Attention Bypass (Bahdanau et al. 2015)

The Extended Mind and External Memory: Active Externalism and the Parity Principle (Clark & Chalmers 1998)
Formulates active externalism: when external artifacts (notebooks, vector databases, search indices) function reliably within a cognitive loop, they constitute literal components of an extended memory system. Attention Scarcity in Information-Rich Organizations (Simon 1971)
Herbert Simon established the foundational economic law of modern knowledge work: in an information-rich world, a wealth of information creates an inevitable poverty of attention, requiring organizations to design filters rather than merely adding storage. Google Effects on Memory: Transactive Memory and Cognitive Offloading (Sparrow et al. 2011)
Controlled experiments demonstrate that when participants expect information to remain accessible via search engines, they show lower recall for the facts themselves but significantly higher recall for where the facts are stored. Episodic Memory, Semantic Lookup, and Autonoetic Consciousness (Tulving 1985)
Endel Tulving established the tripartite classification of memory systems—procedural (anoetic), semantic (noetic), and episodic (autonoetic)—proving that personal recollective mental time travel is neurobiologically distinct from factual knowledge lookup.		 HIGH episodic-memory, semantic-memory, autonoetic-consciousness, mental-time-travel, tulving, philosophy-of-memory HTML PDF
