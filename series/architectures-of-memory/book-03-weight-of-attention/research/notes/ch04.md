## Findings

### Query 1 — Scaled dot-product attention scales query-key products by 1/sqrt(d_k) to prevent softmax gradient saturation.
Vaswani et al. (2017), Section 3.2.1.

### Query 2 — Attention maps query and key-value pairs to an output through packed matrix multiplications.
Vaswani et al. (2017), Section 3.2.
