Differentially Private Stochastic Gradient Descent (Abadi et al. 2016)

Clipping per-sample gradient norms and injecting calibrated Gaussian noise during stochastic gradient descent (DP-SGD) provides provable (epsilon, delta)-differential privacy for deep neural networks.
Status: STABLE Confidence: HIGH Tier: robust_<20% technical Download 2-Page PDF
Quantitative Anchors
differentially private stochastic gradient descent (DP-SGD) with per-sample gradient clippingmoments accountant technique tracking privacy loss across training epochscertified (epsilon = 1.25, delta = 10^-5) privacy on MNIST and CIFAR-10

What We Know (Narrowest Supported Conclusion)

Bounding the influence of individual training examples via per-sample gradient clipping to maximum L₂ norm C and adding calibrated Gaussian noise N(0, σ^2 C^2 I) to mini-batch gradients guarantees (ε, \delta)-differential privacy for deep neural network training under the Moments Accountant framework.

Evidence and Causal Assessment

Mathematical tracking of higher-order moments of privacy loss random variables (the Moments Accountant) establishes tight composition bounds over thousands of training steps, directly enabling non-convex deep learning with rigorous privacy bounds.

Effect Size and Persistence

DP-SGD maintains neural network accuracy within modest degradations (e.g., 5–10% accuracy gap on complex vision/language tasks) while providing mathematical protection against membership inference and model inversion attacks for the entire operational life of the weights.

Bias, Heterogeneity, and Confounding

Classified as robust (<20%). Gradient clipping disproportionately attenuates gradients from under-represented minority classes or rare examples, causing DP-SGD to exhibit disparate impact on model utility across class distributions.

What This Does Not Mean (Strict Boundary Conditions)

Training with DP-SGD does not eliminate utility degradation; strict privacy guarantees (ε < 2) require substantial compute overhead and typically incur notable accuracy reductions relative to unconstrained SGD.

What Remains Uncertain

Scaling DP-SGD efficiently to billion-parameter autoregressive models without severe throughput slowdowns caused by per-sample gradient computation and communication overhead.

What Action Is Reasonably Justified

Use DP-SGD and the Moments Accountant when training deep neural networks on sensitive user records, medical images, or confidential institutional communications.
