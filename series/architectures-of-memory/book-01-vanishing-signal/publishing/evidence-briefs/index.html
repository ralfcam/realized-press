The Dilemma of Long-Term Dependencies in Gradient Descent (Bengio et al. 1994)
Yoshua Bengio, Patrice Simard, and Paolo Frasconi proved that in recurrent neural networks, the mathematical conditions required to robustly store information against noise force error gradients to decay exponentially over time. Habit-Memory, Pure Recollection, and Lived Duration (Bergson 1896)
Bergson's dual memory architecture differentiates motor habit-memory from episodic pure memory, establishing that lived duration is qualitative and continuous rather than a spatialized sequence of clock ticks, directly illuminating the trade-offs of Bergson duration versus spatialized time in AI architectures.		 HIGH bergson, duration, habit-memory, pure-memory, spatialized-time, bergson-duration-versus-spatialized-time-ai HTML PDF
Simple Recurrent Networks and Context Unit Dynamics (Elman 1990)
Context units copying previous hidden states allow recurrent networks to process temporal structure and miniature grammars; memory capacity is fundamentally bounded by un-gated recurrent Jacobian decay across sequence length. The Ship of Theseus and the Bifurcation of Diachronic Identity (Hobbes 1655 & Sider 2001)
Hobbes's two-ships puzzle demonstrates the formal impossibility of unifying diachronic identity across both continuous historical form and original material constitution, providing the foundational conceptual vocabulary for persistence in continually fine-tuned neural models. Vanishing and Exploding Gradients in Recurrent Networks (Hochreiter 1991 & Bengio et al. 1994)
In standard recurrent neural networks trained with gradient descent, error signals propagated across T steps scale exponentially with the spectral radius of the recurrent Jacobian, causing long-term credit assignment to vanish or explode.		 HIGH rnn, vanishing-gradient, backpropagation-through-time HTML PDF
Computational Limitations of Linear Perceptrons: XOR, Locality, and the AI Winter (Minsky & Papert 1969)
Marvin Minsky and Seymour Papert proved that single-layer perceptrons are geometrically incapable of computing non-linearly separable predicates such as parity (XOR) or topological connectedness, precipitating the first connectionist AI Winter.		 HIGH perceptrons, minsky-papert, xor, computational-geometry, ai-winter, connectionism HTML PDF

Organizational knowledge creation requires continuous conversion between tacit and explicit dimensions across four modes (Socialization, Externalization, Combination, Internalization); restructuring disrupts the fragile tacit socialization phase. The Perceptron Convergence Theorem and Linear Separability (Rosenblatt 1958 & Block 1962)
Frank Rosenblatt proved that a single-layer threshold perceptron updating weights via additive error feedback is guaranteed to converge to a separating hyperplane in a finite number of steps if the training dataset is linearly separable.
