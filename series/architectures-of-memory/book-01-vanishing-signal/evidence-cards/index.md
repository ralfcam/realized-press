# Evidence Cards — Book 1: The Vanishing Signal

Twelve OKF v0.3 atomic evidence cards anchoring this volume across technical, organizational, and philosophical lenses:

## Technical Lens (Backpropagation, RNNs & Gradient Dynamics)
* [The Perceptron Convergence Theorem and Linear Separability](rosenblatt-1958-perceptron-convergence-theorem.md) — **`status: stable`** (Grade: High): Rosenblatt 1958 / Block 1962; guaranteed finite-step convergence of linear threshold units on linearly separable patterns.
* [Computational Limitations of Linear Perceptrons: XOR, Locality, and the AI Winter](minsky-papert-1969-perceptrons-xor-locality.md) — **`status: stable`** (Grade: High): Minsky & Papert 1969 / Rumelhart et al. 1986; geometric impossibility of computing non-separable predicates (XOR, connectedness) with single-layer devices.
* [Generalized Delta Rule and Error Backpropagation](rumelhart-1986-backpropagation.md) — **`status: stable`** (Grade: High): Rumelhart, Hinton & Williams 1986 / Werbos 1990; exact multi-layer credit assignment via recursive multivariate chain rule.
* [Simple Recurrent Networks and Context Unit Dynamics](elman-1990-simple-recurrent-networks.md) — **`status: stable`** (Grade: High finite grammar; Low unbounded horizon): Elman 1990 / Bengio 1994; internal dynamic context representation bounded by un-gated Jacobian decay.
* [Vanishing and Exploding Gradients in Recurrent Networks](hochreiter-1991-vanishing-gradient.md) — **`status: stable`** (Grade: High): Hochreiter 1991 / Bengio et al. 1994; exponential decay of backpropagated credit signals with recurrent Jacobian spectral radius.
* [The Dilemma of Long-Term Dependencies in Gradient Descent](bengio-1994-long-term-dependencies-gradient-descent.md) — **`status: stable`** (Grade: High): Bengio, Simard & Frasconi 1994 / Hochreiter 1991; mathematical conflict between hyperbolic attractor noise robustness and exponential gradient decay across temporal lags $T > 10$.
* [Exploding and Vanishing Gradients in Recurrent Neural Networks: Geometry and Norm Clipping](pascanu-2013-exploding-vanishing-gradient-clipping.md) — **`status: draft`** (Grade: High): Pascanu, Mikolov & Bengio 2013; geometric cliffs in RNN error surfaces and gradient norm clipping.

## Organizational Lens (Knowledge Retention, Decay & Reorganizations)
* [Organizational Memory Retention Bins and Retrieval Degradation](walsh-ungson-1991-organizational-memory.md) — **`status: draft`** (Grade: Moderate): Walsh & Ungson 1991 / Eppler & Mengis 2004; five decentralized retention bins and systematic institutional amnesia under turnover.
* [SECI Knowledge Conversion Model and Tacit Transmission Loss](nonaka-1994-seci-knowledge-conversion.md) — **`status: draft`** (Grade: Moderate): Nonaka 1994 / Walsh 1991; socialization-to-internalization conversion spiral and tacit knowledge loss during structural churn.
* [Code Comment and Documentation Reference Decay](tan-2023-code-comment-rot.md) — **`status: stable`** (Grade: Moderate): Tan et al. 2023 / Holtzblatt 2010; empirical decay and contributor hesitation in un-gated software documentation.

## Philosophical Lens (Temporal Phenomenology & Lived Duration)
* [Habit-Memory, Pure Recollection, and Lived Duration](bergson-1896-matter-and-memory-duration.md) — **`status: stable`** (Grade: High): Bergson 1896 / 1889; dual-memory architecture distinguishing somatic habit from dated recollection, and the spatialization of time in learning systems.
* [The Ship of Theseus and the Bifurcation of Diachronic Identity](hobbes-1655-de-corpore-ship-of-theseus.md) — **`status: stable`** (Grade: High): Hobbes 1655 / Sider 2001; dual-logbook puzzle where continuous replacement of parts conflicts with reassembly of original matter.
