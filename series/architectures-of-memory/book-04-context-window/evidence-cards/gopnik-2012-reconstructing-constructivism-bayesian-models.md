# Reconstructing Constructivism: Causal Models and Bayesian Cognition

> Domain: developmental-cognition / causal-models
> Last updated: 2026-10-23
> Status: Stable (definitive developmental psychology framework)

## What We Know

Children do not simply absorb passive statistical associations from environmental input; they construct abstract, coherent, causal representations ("theories") of physical, biological, and psychological domains. Gopnik & Wellman demonstrate that this cognitive development operates via hierarchical Bayesian model selection combined with active exploratory interventions (play and experimentation), generating genuine structural knowledge from sparse, noisy observations.

## How Confident We Are

**Grade**: High

Published in *Psychological Bulletin* (APA), summarizing decades of empirical experiments with infants and toddlers using blicket detectors, causal chain apparatuses, and counterfactual intervention paradigms.

## Evidence and Causal Assessment

Laboratory experiments show that toddlers as young as two years old infer causal directionality, screen off spurious correlations, and design novel physical interventions after observing fewer than half a dozen statistical samples—a sample efficiency orders of magnitude beyond deep neural networks.

## Effect Size and Persistence

Where deep statistical models require billions of tokens to identify correlations that remain fragile to out-of-distribution shifts, human children acquire robust, transferable causal models that support counterfactual reasoning, explanation, and imaginative physical manipulation.

## Bias, Heterogeneity, and Confounding

Classified as `robust (<20%)`. Causal inference mechanisms develop unevenly across domains (intuitive physics precedes complex theory of mind), but the Bayesian structural revision process is ubiquitous across cultures.

## What Remains Uncertain

How human biological neural wetware implements hierarchical Bayesian priors and causal graph structures at the cellular and circuit level.

## What This Does Not Mean

This does not imply that language models possess no utility for modeling human linguistic habits; it indicates that language models operate via passive correlational pattern matching rather than active, embodied causal world-modeling.

## What Action Is Reasonably Justified

Contrast LLM statistical sequence prediction with human causal reasoning when evaluating high-stakes decision systems; do not confuse fluent conversational mimicry with child-like causal understanding.
