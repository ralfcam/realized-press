## Findings

### Query 1 & 2 — Organizational Forget Gates
Drawing an explicit bridge from Gers et al. (2000) to corporate governance, an organization requires formal mechanisms to retire obsolete policies, dead code, and expired procedures. Karl Weick (*Sensemaking in Organizations*, 1995) emphasized that adapting to novel environments requires dropping outdated tools and mental models.
