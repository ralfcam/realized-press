## Findings

### Query 1 — Borges's Funes the Memorious
In "Funes el memorioso" (1942, collected in *Ficciones*, 1944), Jorge Luis Borges portrayed Ireneo Funes, who after a riding injury possessed infallible perception and memory. Borges observed that Funes was incapable of thought because "to think is to forget differences, to generalize, to abstract."

### Query 2 — Luria's Case Study of Shereshevsky
> **Luria, A. R.** *The Mind of a Mnemonist: A Little Book About a Vast Memory*. Translated by L. Solotaroff. New York: Basic Books (1968).
Neuropsychologist Alexander Luria spent thirty years studying Solomon Shereshevsky ('S'), whose vivid synesthesia and unlimited recall severely hindered his ability to understand figurative speech, poetry, or abstract themes.
