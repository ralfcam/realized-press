## Findings

### Query 1 — FDA 21 CFR Part 11 Mandates
Title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations Part 11 establishes criteria under which the FDA considers electronic records and signatures trustworthy and equivalent to paper. It mandates exhaustive, computer-generated, time-stamped audit trails for any modification of records.

### Query 2 — FAA and SOX Retention Mandates
Federal Aviation Administration regulations (14 CFR § 25) require manufacturers to retain complete design, fabrication, and maintenance records for the operational lifespan of commercial aircraft (often 40+ years). Sarbanes-Oxley Act of 2002 Section 802 imposes criminal penalties for altering or destroying audit documents for at least seven years.
