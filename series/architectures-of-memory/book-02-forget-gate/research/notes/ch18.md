## Findings

### Query 1 — Cross-Lens Synthesis
The development of the Long Short-Term Memory cell between 1991 and 2000 uncovered a universal principle across artificial intelligence, organizational design, and cognitive science: representation requires regulation.

### Query 2 — The Architectural Ceiling of Recurrence
Even with adaptive forget gates, recurrent models compress an expanding sequence into a fixed-dimensional cell state $C_t$. This sequential bottleneck eventually spurred the invention of attention mechanisms and the Transformer architecture (Vaswani et al. 2017), which inspects entire sequence horizons simultaneously.
