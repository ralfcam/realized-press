## Findings

### Query 1 — Kirkpatrick et al. (2017) Elastic Weight Consolidation
> **Kirkpatrick, J., et al.** "Overcoming catastrophic forgetting in neural networks." *PNAS* 114, no. 13 (2017): 3521–3526. DOI: 10.1073/pnas.1611835114.
DeepMind researchers introduced Elastic Weight Consolidation (EWC), using the diagonal of the Fisher Information Matrix to constrain updates on weights vital to previous tasks, preventing catastrophic forgetting.

### Query 2 — Differential Privacy in Deep Learning
> **Abadi, M., et al.** "Deep Learning with Differential Privacy." *ACM CCS* (2016): 308–318. DOI: 10.1145/2976749.2978318.
Abadi et al. developed DP-SGD to ensure machine learning models do not memorize sensitive individual training points.

### Query 3 — Plato's Phaedrus
In the *Phaedrus*, Plato recounts the dialogue between King Thamus and the god Theuth: externalizing memory into writing creates the illusion of wisdom while atrophying genuine internal recall.
