## Findings

### Query 1 — Locke's Memory Criterion Revisited
John Locke established that personal identity consists in psychological continuity of consciousness across time.

### Query 2 — Ricoeur's Narrative Identity
In *Time and Narrative* and *Oneself as Another* (1992), Ricoeur distinguished between *idem* identity (sameness of material substance) and *ipse* identity (selfhood maintained through narrative commitments and promises).

### Query 3 — Derek Parfit's Psychological Continuity
> **Parfit, D.** *Reasons and Persons*. Oxford: Clarendon Press (1984).
Parfit argued that personal survival is grounded in overlapping chains of psychological connectedness and continuity (Relation R), rather than indivisible ego substance.
