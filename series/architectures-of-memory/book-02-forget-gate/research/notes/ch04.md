## Findings

### Query 1 & 2 — Gers's Forget Gate
Felix A. Gers, together with Jürgen Schmidhuber and Fred Cummins, introduced the forget gate in 1999 (at ICANN) and published the canonical journal paper in 2000:
> **Gers, F. A., Schmidhuber, J., and Cummins, F.** "Learning to Forget: Continual Prediction with LSTM." *Neural Computation* 12, no. 10 (2000): 2451–2471. DOI: 10.1162/089976600300015015.

The forget gate scales the previous cell state $C_{t-1}$ by an element-wise factor $f_t = \sigma(W_f x_t + U_f h_{t-1} + b_f)$, allowing the network to retain, decay, or reset the memory vector dynamically.

### Query 3 — Holan & Phillips (2004) Organizational Forgetting
> **de Holan, P. M., and Phillips, N.** "Remembrance of Things Past? The Dynamics of Organizational Forgetting." *Management Science* 50, no. 11 (2004): 1603–1613. DOI: 10.1287/mnsc.1040.0273.
Holan and Phillips identified four modes of organizational forgetting: memory decay, failure to capture, unlearning, and managed forgetting. They demonstrated that organizational survival depends on active unlearning rather than passive accumulation.
