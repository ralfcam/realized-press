## Findings

### Query 1 — Holan & Phillips on Unmanaged Accumulation
de Holan & Phillips (2004) demonstrated that organizations that fail to curate their knowledge bases suffer from cognitive congestion. Storing every operational artifact without pruning creates an archive where high-value signals are drowned out by noise.

### Query 2 — Michael Power's Audit Society
> **Power, M.** *The Audit Society: Rituals of Verification*. Oxford: Oxford University Press (1997).
Power showed that regulatory regimes often mandate documentation for legal defensibility rather than operational effectiveness, leading to compliance theater.
