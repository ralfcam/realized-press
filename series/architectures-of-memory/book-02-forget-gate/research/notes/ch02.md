## Findings

### Query 1 & 2 — The Constant Error Carousel (CEC)
The Constant Error Carousel (CEC) is the central linear unit within an LSTM memory cell. By giving the unit a recurrent self-connection with a fixed weight of 1.0 and an identity activation function whose derivative $f'(net) = 1.0$, the local error carousel ensures that backpropagated gradients flow across arbitrary time steps without exponential scaling:
$$f'_{j}(net_j(t)) = 1.0, \quad w_{jj} = 1.0 \implies artheta_j(t) = artheta_j(t-1)$$

### Query 3 — Pascanu, Mikolov, and Bengio (2013)
> **Pascanu, R., Mikolov, T., and Bengio, Y.** "On the difficulty of training recurrent neural networks." In *International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML)*, pp. 1310–1318 (2013).
This work rigorously unpacked the geometry of exploding and vanishing gradients, verifying Hochreiter's 1991 theorem and providing the formal geometric justification for norm clipping and linear error carousels.

### Query 4 — Bergsonian Duration and Computational Time
Henri Bergson argued in *Time and Free Will* (1889) that treating time as a series of juxtaposed discrete points falsifies conscious experience. The CEC establishes a computational analog of pure duration by preserving continuous scalar states across sequence steps.
