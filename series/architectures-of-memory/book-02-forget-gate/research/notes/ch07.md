## Findings

### Query 1 — Gers's Peephole Connections
Gers and Schmidhuber (IJCNN 2000) added peephole connections allowing the gates to directly inspect the current cell state $C_{t-1}$ without waiting for the output gate to open:
> **Gers, F. A., and Schmidhuber, J.** "Recurrent nets that time and count." *IJCNN* (2000). DOI: 10.1109/IJCNN.2000.861302.

### Query 2 — Bidirectional RNNs
> **Schuster, M., and Paliwal, K. K.** "Bidirectional recurrent neural networks." *IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing* 45, no. 11 (1997): 2673–2681. DOI: 10.1109/78.650093.

### Query 3 — Gated Recurrent Units (GRU)
> **Cho, K., et al.** "Learning Phrase Representations using RNN Encoder-Decoder for Statistical Machine Translation." *EMNLP* (2014): 1724–1734. DOI: 10.3115/v1/D14-1179.
Cho et al. eliminated the separate cell state, merging the cell and hidden state into a single vector modulated by reset and update gates.

### Query 4 — Merleau-Ponty's Double Horizon
In *Phénoménologie de la perception* (1945), Merleau-Ponty describes lived time as spanning retention of the immediate past and protention of the anticipated future, providing a philosophical mirror to bidirectional sequence models.
