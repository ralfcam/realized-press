## Findings

### Query 1 — Connectionist Temporal Classification (CTC)
Alex Graves et al. (ICML 2006) introduced Connectionist Temporal Classification (CTC), allowing recurrent networks to be trained directly on unsegmented sequence pairs, eliminating the need for pre-aligned phonetic targets.

### Query 2 & 3 — Production Scale: Google Voice & GNMT
By 2015, Google replaced its acoustic models for Google Voice with deep LSTMs, reporting a 49% drop in word error rate. In 2016, Google deployed the Google Neural Machine Translation (GNMT) system:
> **Wu, Y., et al.** "Google's Neural Machine Translation System: Bridging the Gap between Human and Machine Translation." *arXiv:1609.08144* (2016).
GNMT used an 8-layer deep LSTM encoder and 8-layer LSTM decoder with residual connections.

### Query 4 — Linda Argote's Industrial Forgetting Curves
> **Argote, L., Beckman, S. L., and Epple, D.** "The Persistence and Transfer of Learning in Industrial Settings." *Management Science* 36, no. 2 (1990): 140–154. DOI: 10.1287/mnsc.36.2.140.
Argote documented that production knowledge acquired in manufacturing settings depreciates rapidly without active reinforcement, establishing an empirical counterpart to neural forgetting.
