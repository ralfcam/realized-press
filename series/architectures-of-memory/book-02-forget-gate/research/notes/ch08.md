## Findings

### Query 1 & 2 — Argote's Research on Knowledge Depreciation
Linda Argote's extensive studies (1990, 1999) confirm that organizational knowledge decays significantly over time unless formalized into tools, routines, and culture:
> **Argote, L.** *Organizational Learning: Creating, Retaining and Transferring Knowledge*. Norwell, MA: Kluwer Academic Publishers (1999).

When staff depart without active knowledge transfer, subsequent teams cannot reconstruct the rationale behind design choices, turning software architectures into legacy black boxes.
