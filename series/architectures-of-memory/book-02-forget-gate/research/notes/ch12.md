## Findings

### Query 1 — Paul Ricoeur's Ethics of Memory
> **Ricoeur, P.** *Memory, History, Forgetting*. Translated by K. Blamey and D. Pellauer. Chicago: University of Chicago Press (2004).
Ricoeur argued that ethical remembrance requires maintaining a truthful, accountable historical narrative while acknowledging the necessity of forgiveness and closure.

### Query 2 — Engineering Change Orders (ECO)
In mechanical, aerospace, and software engineering, formal change orders document the causal justification for replacing or deprecating a component, preventing silent drift and maintaining accountability.
