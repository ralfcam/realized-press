## Findings

### Query 1 — GDPR Article 17
Article 17 of the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) establishes the "Right to Erasure" (Right to be Forgotten), requiring data controllers to delete personal data without undue delay under specified conditions.

### Query 2 — Machine Unlearning (SISA)
> **Bourtoule, L., et al.** "Machine Unlearning." *IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy (S&P)* (2021): 141–159. DOI: 10.1109/SP40001.2021.00019.
Bourtoule et al. introduced the SISA (Sharded, Isolated, Sliced, Aggregated) architecture, enabling efficient retraining of models when individual user data must be excised.
