# Toddler Peer Interaction Is Not a Social-Skills Commodity

> Domain: social development
> Last updated: 2026-09-16
> Status: Source-audited first pass (CH08-C04)

## What We Know

A caregiver is usually highly motivated to understand and adapt to a child. Another one- or two-year-old is not. Brownell, Ramani & Zerwas (2006) showed that coordinated peer activity (for example, cooperating to work a toy) is limited at one year and becomes more possible across the second year as social understanding and shared goals come online. Early peer life is therefore a context with distinctive demands — imitation, object conflict, repair — not a background object and not a purchased skill.

## How Confident We Are

**Grade**: Moderate that peer contexts create reciprocal coordination problems that adult-only settings do not fully reproduce. **Low** that more peer hours cause later trust, popularity, or moral character, or that peer exposure in infancy is the same process as school-age friendship.

## Evidence and Causal Assessment

Laboratory and structured-play observations, not randomization to “more peers.” Child temperament, existing competence, and adult scaffolding select who plays with whom. Later longitudinal links from preschool peer quality to prosocial behavior are transactional and are not this card’s claim.

## Effect Size and Persistence

No portable standardized effect for “peer exposure.” The defensible unit is age-typical change in coordinated activity under specified tasks.

## Bias, Heterogeneity, and Confounding

GI attenuation: **n/a**. Group composition, adult scaffolding, and which child already has more skill all confound “peer effects.” Aggressive or chaotic groups are a different exposure.

## What Remains Uncertain

When reliable, independent peer influence begins; how sibling interaction relates to extrafamilial peers; cultural variation in when peers are ordinary companions.

## What This Does Not Mean

Does not mean peer exposure automatically improves social skills. Does not mean an only child is deprived. Does not mean stranger wariness is a peer-skills failure. Does not mean group care is institutional deprivation (`severe-institutional-deprivation.md`).

## What Action Is Reasonably Justified

Treat safe, reciprocal play with other children as a possible context, not a commodity. Quality, safety, and adult support matter more than manufacturing a peer schedule.
