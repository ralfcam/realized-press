# Fetal Growth Restriction Is a Clinical Outcome, Not a Destiny

> Domain: pregnancy
> Last updated: 2026-09-15
> Status: Source-audited first pass (CH02-C12; C13 interpretive continuity held on this card)

## What We Know

Fetal growth reflects genes, placenta, maternal condition, and environment. INTERGROWTH-21st constructed international ultrasound standards from low-risk pregnancies so that size can be interpreted against a common chart. Severe restriction is clinically meaningful. Birth size is not intelligence, worth, or personality. Prenatal differences can attenuate, persist, or change form after birth; persistence must be measured (CH02-C13).

## How Confident We Are

**Grade**: Moderate that international standards improve measurement comparability; **High** as interpretation that percentile ≠ destiny. Catch-up is common among many term infants and does not erase every risk.

## Evidence and Causal Assessment

Papageorghiou et al. (2014), INTERGROWTH-21st Fetal Growth Longitudinal Study: 4,321 low-risk pregnancies across eight countries; serial ultrasound from 14 weeks; centiles for head circumference, BPD, OFD, abdominal circumference, femur length. This is a standards paper, not a trial of smallness. Continuity after birth is a longitudinal claim: attenuation, persistence, and transformation are all possible; none can be assumed from size alone.

## Effect Size and Persistence

Distinguish percentile difference, functional difference, and impairment. Dating error and constitutional smallness are not placental failure.

## Bias, Heterogeneity, and Confounding

GI attenuation: **n/a** (normative anthropometric measurement standard, not an observational exposure).

Low-risk INTERGROWTH sample is prescriptive, not a census of all fetuses; collider/selection if only small babies are followed; postnatal nutrition.

## What Remains Uncertain

Which restricted fetuses will show which postnatal trajectories, and which interventions change those trajectories.

## What This Does Not Mean

Does not mean a small newborn is a lesser child, or that catch-up proves the prenatal period was irrelevant.

## What Action Is Reasonably Justified

Clinical surveillance of true growth restriction is justified. Treating a percentile as a forecast of character is not.
