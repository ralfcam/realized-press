# Early Skin-to-Skin Contact: Immediate Physiology, Not Character

> Domain: birth / feeding
> Last updated: 2026-09-15
> Status: Source-audited first pass (CH03-C06)

## What We Know

Early skin-to-skin contact (naked infant prone on the mother’s chest soon after birth) is a bundled care practice, not a single dose of affection. In healthy, mostly term newborns, Cochrane synthesis finds more breastfeeding at one to four months and mixed, lower-certainty infant physiologic results. Kangaroo mother care for low-birth-weight infants is a different population and must not be averaged into this card.

## How Confident We Are

**Grade**: Moderate for any breastfeeding at 1–4 months (RR 1.24, 95% CI 1.07–1.43; 14 trials, 887 women; GRADE moderate). **Low** for infant blood glucose (MD +10.49 mg/dL; 3 trials, 144 infants) and for temperature (MD +0.30 °C; 6 trials, 558 infants; not clinically meaningful; GRADE low). **Low** for SCRIP stabilization (2 trials, 81 infants). **Low** that SSC determines bonding, personality, or later cognition.

## Evidence and Causal Assessment

Moore, Bergman, Anderson & Medley (2016): 38 trials with data (3,472 women); most small; none met all good-quality criteria; no trial was blinded. Comparator is usual hospital care. This is an RCT synthesis of a bundled, unblindable intervention. Benefits cannot be assigned to parental love alone when warmth, feeding support, and reduced separation travel together.

## Effect Size and Persistence

Breastfeeding maintenance is the strongest identified outcome here. Duration findings are smaller-sample and GRADE low. Infant temperature difference is statistically present and clinically trivial. Long-term neurodevelopment is not established by this review.

## Bias, Heterogeneity, and Confounding

GI attenuation: **n/a** (immediate postpartum clinical physiology and RCT trials, not an observational phenotype).

Performance bias is structural (the intervention is visible). Small samples, co-interventions, and heterogeneous protocols. SSC is not KMC; mixing term SSC with mortality results from very-low-birth-weight kangaroo care is a translation error.

## What Remains Uncertain

Dose (timing, duration, who holds the infant), cesarean-specific effects, and whether short-term physiology translates into later development.

## What This Does Not Mean

Does not mean SSC guarantees bonding, breastfeeding, or cognitive advantage, or that its absence is a character injury. It does not license treating contact as mandatory moral performance.

## What Action Is Reasonably Justified

Make safe, supported contact available as part of essential newborn care. Moralizing contact, or treating a missed hour as a life sentence, is not justified by these trials.
