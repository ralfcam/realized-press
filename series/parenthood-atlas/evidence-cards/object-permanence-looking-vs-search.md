# Object Permanence Is Not a Single Staircase

> Domain: early learning
> Last updated: 2026-09-15
> Status: Source-audited first pass (CH05-C06). Adjacent looking-time method card: `looking-time-as-measurement.md`.

## What We Know

Piaget’s search tasks placed usable object permanence near the end of the first year because they required coordinated action. Baillargeon (1987) used a drawbridge/occlusion looking-time design: 4.5-month-olds, and some 3.5-month-olds, looked longer at an “impossible” rotation through the space of a hidden box than at a possible rotation. That is evidence of sensitivity to an occlusion event in a looking measure. It is not by itself a complete, action-ready object concept.

## How Confident We Are

**Grade**: Moderate that young infants can show looking-time sensitivity to persistence/solidity displays of this kind. **Low** that the look is a finished theory of objects, that Piaget’s search ages were simply measurement error, or that a single staircase is acquired once.

## Evidence and Causal Assessment

Baillargeon (1987), *Developmental Psychology*: experimental contrast of possible vs impossible events after habituation, with a control condition that lacked the box. Identification is looking-time, not search. Later commentary (including Aslin 2007 and subsequent VoE critiques) treats linking hypotheses as open. Competence and performance can diverge: an infant may look longer at an impossible event and still fail a manual search that requires means–end coordination.

## Effect Size and Persistence

The outcome is contemporaneous looking, not later physics competence. Persistence of a “concept” across tasks is exactly what this literature does not settle.

## Bias, Heterogeneity, and Confounding

GI attenuation: **n/a** (laboratory cognitive developmental task, not an observational population phenotype association).

Salience, rotation extent, habituation criteria, and trial structure can change looking. Small classic samples. Replication and alternative perceptual accounts remain live. Task demands (looking vs reaching vs multiple objects) are not noise; they are part of the claim.

## What Remains Uncertain

How much of the original drawbridge effect is conceptual versus perceptual; which object expectations survive multi-lab replication; how representations become usable in action.

## What This Does Not Mean

Does not mean young infants are miniature physicists. Does not mean Piaget should be discarded as a whole. Does not mean a missed peekaboo writes a cognitive fate. Core-knowledge slogans are not this card.

## What Action Is Reasonably Justified

Ordinary play with disappearing and reappearing objects is consistent with the finding. Object-permanence toys as cognitive enhancement are not justified.
