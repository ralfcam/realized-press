# Childhood Self-Control Is a Population Gradient, Not a Sentence

> Domain: age-3 waypoint / self-control
> Last updated: 2026-09-16
> Status: Source-audited first pass (CH15-C07). Watts delay, EF, and inhibitory-control cards are neighbors, not rewritten.

## What We Know

Moffitt et al. (2011) followed the Dunedin birth cohort: a composite of childhood self-control (ages 3–11) predicted adult health, wealth, and public-safety outcomes, including in sibling comparisons. Children whose self-control *improved* across childhood had better later outcomes than their early standing implied. The object is a population gradient, not a single laboratory delay trial at age four.

## How Confident We Are

**Grade**: Moderate that childhood self-control, as measured in this cohort, is a meaningful group-level predictor after some covariates. **Low** that a three-year-old’s delay or compliance score forecasts that child’s adult life, or that the original marshmallow-test predictor claim is hereby restored.

## Evidence and Causal Assessment

Single-cohort longitudinal design with sibling checks. Not genetically informed attenuation in the CONTRIBUTING sense on this card. The Watts 2018 NICHD replication (`self-regulation-delay-of-gratification.md`) already showed that the classic marshmallow *school-success* story was mostly confounded; this card does not reopen that identification. Adjacent Chapter 11 cards describe toddler inhibition as a task family, not a life-course law.

## Effect Size and Persistence

Effects are described as a gradient across the distribution, not only the clinical tail. They are modest relative to a life, and they can move when self-control changes.

## Bias, Heterogeneity, and Confounding

GI attenuation: **untested-in-GI** (childhood self-control is moderately heritable and shares genetic variance with socioeconomic gradients; specific genetically informed attenuation studies on Dunedin gradient estimates remain unparameterized on this card).

Family resources, IQ, and later environments remain entangled. Childhood self-control here is a multi-age composite, not a 36-month snapshot.

## What Remains Uncertain

How much of the adult association is the child’s later environments continuing the same constraints that produced early self-control.

## What This Does Not Mean

Low self-control at three does not doom a child. This is not a marshmallow-test replication. It does not justify ranking toddlers or converting ordinary life into a self-control curriculum.

## What Action Is Reasonably Justified

Scaffold regulation and reduce cumulative risk at the population level. Individual destiny language does not follow.
