# Prenatal Environmental Mixtures and Placental Markers

> Domain: pregnancy
> Last updated: 2026-09-15
> Status: Source-audited first pass (CH02-C11)

## What We Know

Many chemicals can reach or alter the placenta. In SEPAGES, selected phthalate metabolites (notably MBzP) were associated with lower placental weight and related markers. Phenol mixtures in the same analysis were not associated. This is a placental-marker claim, not proven child neurodevelopmental injury from typical consumer detection.

## How Confident We Are

**Grade**: Low for specific phthalate–placenta associations in one well-measured cohort; **Very Low** that typical mixture detection causes permanent child injury or that household purification is the implied action.

## Evidence and Causal Assessment

Philippat et al. (2024), SEPAGES (France): repeated pooled urines (median 21 samples/trimester), linear models and BKMR. MBzP associated with lower placental weight (about −20 g and −17 g per ln-unit in second and third trimesters) and placental-to-fetal ratio; MnBP with lower vascular resistance; DiNP associations mainly in male infants. No overall phenol-mixture association. Observational environmental epidemiology; not randomized, not genetically informed.

## Effect Size and Persistence

Placental weight shifts are small relative to clinical pathology. Child neurodevelopment was not the outcome here. Persistence and reversibility are unestablished.

## Bias, Heterogeneity, and Confounding

GI attenuation: **n/a** (environmental toxicological biomarker study, not an observational population phenotype association).

SES and housing; multiple-exposure; sex interaction; mechanism-to-outcome leap; single-cohort replication still thin.

## What Remains Uncertain

Fetal dose, thresholds, and whether placental markers mediate later function.

## What This Does Not Mean

Does not mean every detected chemical is an injury, or that parents must purify a household to protect a child’s future.

## What Action Is Reasonably Justified

Institutional chemical-policy and product standards can be discussed at the system level. Individual purity projects are not licensed by these associations.
